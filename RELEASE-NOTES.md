# Folio 1.1.1

Folio continues the existing application with improvements to restoration, credentials and privacy.

- Backups are authenticated, decrypted and checked for supported financial data before import. Replacement requires confirmation and rejects a destination changed since verification.
- New and changed passwords require at least 12 characters. Stronger password protection is used for new credentials, while historical profiles and backups remain supported.
- Background concealment and an explicit failed-lock resolution screen help keep retained work private.
- Android offers optional screenshot protection and protects recent-app previews where supported.
- Backup testing and restore share file-size and JSON-nesting safeguards.
- Help now explains current recovery, privacy, export and financial-planning limits.

Use Folio 1.1.1 or a compatible newer version for backups made with the stronger credential policy. Keep historical backups and their historical passwords or recovery credentials.

This delivery is an unsigned Android release build for owner signing and manual acceptance. Installed-upgrade verification was omitted at the owner's request. See VERIFICATION.md for exact evidence and unresolved items.
